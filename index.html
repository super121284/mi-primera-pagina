THE SPEAKER #01 INTERVIEW BY: CASTRO
06:00 AM // FORMATION 👟

CADET MATEO, 19

TWO DIFFERENT SHOES AT 0600!

Mateo has had several crazy mornings at the academy, and his wildest plot twist happened during an early inspection. He got dressed in total darkness and marched into formation wearing one black boot and one running sneaker! At first, he felt shocked when his squad spotted it, but everyone burst out laughing, so he smiled and owned the mistake.

“I have never gotten ready faster in the dark, and I have checked my feet twice every morning since!”
THE SPEAKER #02 INTERVIEW BY: GARCÍA
WEEKEND LEAVE // STORM ⛈️

CADET SOFÍA, 20

TRAPPED IN A CARIBBEAN STORM!

Sofía has never forgotten a sudden tropical storm that hit Cartagena during her weekend leave. The sky turned pitch black in three minutes, and she ran soaked into a tiny harbor café. She felt annoyed at first, yet she ended up sharing coffee with two retired captains who told hilarious sea legends!

“The rain surprised everyone on the street, but hiding inside turned a ruined afternoon into an epic memory!”
THE SPEAKER #03 INTERVIEW BY: HERRERA
BUS TERMINAL // CLONE 🤯

CADET DANIEL, 21

MEETING HIS TWIN STRANGER!

Daniel has experienced bizarre coincidences while traveling, and his craziest one occurred at a bus station last year. He sat next to a guy who carried the exact same backpack and was reading the exact same navigation book! Daniel froze in total disbelief, laughed out loud, and chatted with his "twin" the whole ride.

“I have seen weird coincidences before, but finding my exact bag and book next to me felt like a glitch in the matrix!”
