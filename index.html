⚓ INTERVIEWER 01: CADET CASTRO

3 INTERVIEWS COMPLETED
CADET #01 BY: CASTRO
06:00 AM // FORMATION 👟

CADET MATEO, 19

TWO DIFFERENT SHOES AT 0600!

Mateo has had several crazy mornings at the academy, and his wildest plot twist happened during an early inspection. He got dressed in total darkness and marched into formation wearing one black boot and one sneaker! He felt shocked when his squad spotted it, but everyone laughed, so he smiled and owned it.

“I have never gotten ready faster in the dark, and I have checked my feet twice every morning since!”
CADET #02 BY: CASTRO
CLASSROOM // WRONG ROOM 🚪

CADET VALERIA, 20

PRESENTING IN THE WRONG CLASS!

Valeria has never forgotten the day she walked late into an auditorium and started setting up her slides. After two minutes, she realized it was a senior engineering lecture! She turned bright red, apologized politely to the captain, and sprinted to her real classroom.

“I have always checked the room number twice since that embarrassing afternoon!”
CADET #03 BY: CASTRO
MESS HALL // SALT SURPRISE 🧂

CADET SANTIAGO, 21

SALT INSTEAD OF SUGAR IN COFFEE!

Santiago has drunk hundreds of cups of coffee before watch duty, but one night he poured two spoons of salt into his mug by mistake. He took a huge sip, spit it out immediately, and woke up his whole table with his reaction!

“I have tasted seawater during training, and that coffee was honestly worse!”

⚓ INTERVIEWER 02: CADET GARCÍA

3 INTERVIEWS COMPLETED
CADET #04 BY: GARCÍA
WEEKEND LEAVE // STORM ⛈️

CADET SOFÍA, 20

TRAPPED IN A CARIBBEAN STORM!

Sofía has never forgotten a sudden tropical storm that hit Cartagena during her weekend leave. The sky turned black in three minutes, and she ran soaked into a tiny harbor café. She felt annoyed at first, yet she ended up sharing coffee with two retired captains!

“The rain surprised everyone on the street, but hiding inside turned a ruined afternoon into an epic memory!”
CADET #05 BY: GARCÍA
HARBOR // DOLPHIN ESCORT 🐬

CADET ANDRÉS, 22

DOLPHINS RACING OUR TRAINING BOAT!

Andrés has sailed around Cartagena Bay many times, and his coolest surprise occurred during a routine navigation drill. Five wild dolphins appeared beside the bow and jumped next to the boat for ten minutes! Everyone cheered and recorded the moment.

“I have seen marine life before, but racing dolphins in full uniform felt completely surreal!”
CADET #06 BY: GARCÍA
PARADE // LOST CAP 💨

CADET CAMILA, 19

SEA BREEZE STOLE HER WHITE CAP!

Camila has marched in dozens of ceremonies, yet one gusty afternoon a strong Caribbean wind blew her white cap across the parade deck! A teammate caught it mid-air without breaking formation, and Camila kept a straight face until dismissal.

“I have never held onto my cap tighter than I did after that ninja catch!”

⚓ INTERVIEWER 03: CADET HERRERA

3 INTERVIEWS COMPLETED
CADET #07 BY: HERRERA
BUS TERMINAL // CLONE 🤯

CADET DANIEL, 21

MEETING HIS TWIN STRANGER!

Daniel has experienced bizarre coincidences while traveling, and his craziest one occurred at a bus station last year. He sat next to a guy who carried the exact same backpack and was reading the same navigation book! Daniel froze in disbelief and chatted with him all trip.

“I have seen weird coincidences before, but finding my exact bag and book felt like a glitch in the matrix!”
CADET #08 BY: HERRERA
AIRPORT // CELEBRITY 📸

CADET LAURA, 20

TOURISTS ASKED FOR A SELFIE!

Laura has worn her white gala uniform on travel days, and once at the airport a group of foreign tourists thought she was a movie actress! She laughed, explained she was a naval cadet, and still took five photos with them.

“I have never felt more famous than I did waiting at Gate 4 in uniform!”
CADET #09 BY: HERRERA
SIMULATOR // PLOT TWIST ⚓

CADET NICOLÁS, 21

PASSED THE TEST ON A POWER CUT!

Nicolás has practiced bridge maneuvers for months, and during his final simulator test the screens suddenly went blank! Instead of panicking, he used his magnetic compass and manual charts, docked safely, and earned top marks.

“I have trusted electronics all semester, so saving the maneuver manually was the ultimate plot twist!”
